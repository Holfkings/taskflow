FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Write to us at hello@taskflow.io

Do I need a credit card to start the free trial?

No. The 14-day Pro trial and the Starter plan both work without a credit card. You only add payment details if you decide to upgrade or stay on Pro.

Can I import my tasks from other tools?

Yes. One-click importers are available for Trello, Asana, Monday, Jira, Todoist and plain CSV, keeping assignees, labels, due dates and attachments intact.

How does per-user pricing work?

Pro is billed at $12 per user per month, based on active members. Guests you invite to review or comment are free and unlimited, and you're only charged for the days a member is active.

Is my data secure and backed up?

Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), with daily backups and a 99.9% uptime SLA on the Enterprise plan. Enterprise customers can also enable SSO, SCIM and audit logs.

Can I cancel or change my plan later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. You can export all your data in CSV or JSON whenever you want, no questions asked.
